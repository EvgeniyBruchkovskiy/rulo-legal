Privacy Policy

Rulo: Logic Puzzle Daily · Effective 1 October 2026

In short: Rulo does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no servers of my own. What you do in the game stays on your device.

Who I am

Rulo: Logic Puzzle Daily (“Rulo”, “the app”) is developed and published by Yevhenii Bruchkovskyi, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). Questions about this policy: thewayprod1@gmail.com.

What I don’t collect

  • No name, email, phone number or other contact details. There is no sign-up or account.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools inside the app.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • Your game data is never sent to any server operated by me.

Data stored on your device

Your progress — solved rules, streak, lesson progress, unlocked items and settings such as theme or colorblind patterns — is saved locally using Apple’s standard storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). A small part of it, such as today’s puzzle status and your streak, is shared with the Rulo home-screen widget through an App Group on the same device.

I cannot see or access this data. It is deleted when you delete the app, and it may be included in your iPhone backups according to your own Apple settings.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, Rulo submits your score for the daily rule to a Game Center leaderboard. This is handled by Apple and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Other players may see your Game Center nickname and score, depending on your Game Center settings. I don’t receive your Apple ID or contact details. If you are not signed in, nothing is submitted.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. I never receive your name, card or billing details. The app only asks Apple’s StoreKit, on your device, which items you own so it can unlock them.

Duel links

A duel link contains only a puzzle code and the number of checks used. It holds no name, identifier or other personal data, and you decide whom to share it with. The duel web page that opens the link runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing.

This website

These pages are static and use no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. The hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability; I do not use them to identify anyone.

Children

Rulo is rated 4+. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children. Game Center and purchases on a child’s device can be managed with Apple’s parental controls (Screen Time, Family Sharing, Ask to Buy).

Your rights

Since I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on my side — wherever you live, including under the GDPR and similar laws. You can erase all game data at any time by deleting the app, and manage Game Center data in your Apple settings. If you email me, your message is used only to reply and will be deleted on request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
